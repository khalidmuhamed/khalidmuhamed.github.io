Spec. 04 / Training project

Sales Force Effectiveness (SFE) Dashboard

Power BIDAXPharma Commercial

An SFE dashboard for pharmaceutical commercial operations. Sales alone doesn't tell the whole story, so I chose KPIs that connect commercial outcomes with field execution:

  • Sales vs. target and achievement rate
  • Demand vs. units sold
  • Doctor coverage and field visits
  • Samples distributed
  • Market share and distributor sales
  • Incentive payouts

I also connected Power BI to an MCP workflow to automate parts of the analytics process: inspecting the semantic model, creating DAX measures and validating report components. The lesson: a useful dashboard depends on clear KPI definitions and metrics that reflect how the business actually works.

A training project built on simulated data, not real company figures.
